Mughal embroidery motifs

Exported: 2026-08-18 20:21:34

Mughal embroidery motifs are heavily derived from architectural decorations such as the pietra dura inlay work found on monuments like the Taj Mahal [1]. The zardozi technique, using metallic threads of gold and silver, reached its peak under Emperor Akbar, who wore elaborate angrakhas embroidered with floral patterns [2]. Common motifs include cypress trees, stylized poppy flowers, and scrolling vines (beli).
